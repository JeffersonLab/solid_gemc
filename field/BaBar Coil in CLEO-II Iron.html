BaBar coil in CLEO-II iron

The BaBar coil (3 sections, 5.119 MA-turns) and its aluminium cryostat, placed in the CLEO-II iron of CLEOv10. The coil radius and the cryostat match NIM A 479 Table 4; the coil is 5.7 cm shorter than the NIM current sheet.

Geometry

  • Cryostat outer radius 177.0 cm, barrel bore 180.0 cm: 3.0 cm gap

Table 4 consistency: ½LI² = 27.1 MJ against the listed 27 MJ stored energy. The 1.5 T central field is for BaBar's own flux return, so it does not apply to this iron.

The cryostat is a vacuum vessel: an aluminium support cylinder, heat shield and housing around evacuated spaces, about 12.6 cm of aluminium in total. POISSON treats aluminium and vacuum alike (μ = 1, no current), so the solid cryostat region gives the same field and forces as a hollow one.

Cold dimensions: the 1995 design shrinks by 0.43 % from warm to cold. Applied to the as-built coil (351.2 cm long, mean radius 153.0 cm at room temperature) that gives about 349.7 cm and 152.3 cm at 4.5 K.
